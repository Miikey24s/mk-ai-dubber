## Reference-selection cache regression

`reference_selection` previously computed its stage fingerprint with policy `3` but committed the manifest with policy `2`. Resume therefore could miss an otherwise valid reference-selection cache.

The stage now uses one shared `REFERENCE_SELECTION_POLICY_VERSION = 3` value for both fingerprint and commit. Focused cache regression: `2 passed`.
